Click a column to sort. Deviation = actual return minus the expected move to target, in percentage points.

Entry = the price at which the idea was posted (the same entry price shown on the Trade Ideas page). Expected move = target ÷ entry − 1 (reference objective for momentum ideas, which are not closed there). Best / worst = highest high and lowest low since entry, from daily bars. Progress is measured after costs (the expected move is reduced by the same charges as the actual result) and capped at ±100%. Figures update when the page loads; live prices refresh every minute in market hours.
