How it works After each close TrendLens ranks the NIFTY 500 on risk-adjusted 12-month momentum (12-month return divided by volatility; stocks trading under Rs 10 cr a day or priced under Rs 50 are left out) and scans for one daily setup, the bull flag (a 15% rise in 10 sessions, then a 3–10 session pause). A committee reports on each candidate: technical, fundamentals, news, macro and NSE surveillance. A coded judge, plain fixed rules with no AI, scores it, counting each piece of evidence once. The CEO agent takes the final call: post or decline, with a written memo. Momentum ideas have their own 20 slots and enter at the next session's open, as in the back-test; setup ideas have their own few slots, skip stocks with results due inside the holding period, and are cancelled if the actual fill leaves less than 2:1 reward-to-risk. New ideas are chosen only on closing prices; during market hours prices are checked every 5 minutes for entries, stop-losses and targets. An idea stays in Live ideas until the CEO closes it: at the stop-loss or target, when the time frame ends, or, for momentum ideas, when the rank falls below #50. A stop hit while a stock is locked at its lower circuit is closed at the next tradable price. The Scorecard shows results after costs (brokerage, STT, exchange, GST, stamp and 0.1% slippage each side) against the NIFTY 500. Back-test of the momentum rules (re-tested Nov 2026, after costs): 21.2% a year in 2011–19 and 45.4% in 2020–26, against 7.9% and 13.8% for the NIFTY 500 index, with falls of up to 28% and 38%; about half the ideas were profitable. It uses today's index members, so stocks that were dropped or delisted are missing and the figures are flattered; a realistic expectation is about 18–25% a year with drops of 30–40%. The bull-flag setup back-tested at a small edge after costs (+0.07 of the risk per trade over 2,588 trades, 44% profitable); 20-day breakouts lost money after costs and are no longer posted. Past results do not guarantee future ones. Consult a SEBI-registered adviser before investing.
